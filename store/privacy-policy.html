Nightbound

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 5, 2026
App: Nightbound (package com.nightbound.app)
Contact: rinpan@yahoo.com

Nightbound helps adults unwind with soft literary bedtime stories. We collect only what we need to run the app, generate stories, and manage subscriptions. We do not sell your personal information.

1. Who we are

“Nightbound,” “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to the developer of the Nightbound mobile application. For privacy questions, email rinpan@yahoo.com.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the app, we may process:

  • Device & app data — device type, OS version, app version, crash logs, and basic diagnostics.
  • Usage data — features used (e.g. mood selected, story generated, paywall views), timestamps, and free-tier usage counts.
  • Story content — stories you generate, optional short prompts/seeds you provide, favorites, and library items stored on device and/or on our servers if you use cloud features.
  • Account data (if sign-in is enabled) — identifiers from your sign-in provider (e.g. Google account ID and email).
  • Purchase data — subscription status and transaction tokens from Google Play Billing. We do not receive your full payment card number; Google processes payments.
  • Support messages — information you send us by email.

On-device text-to-speech uses your device’s system voice engine. Audio is typically synthesized on device and is not uploaded by us solely for TTS.

3. How we use information

  • Provide and improve story generation and the reading/listening experience.
  • Enforce free-tier limits and premium entitlements.
  • Process subscriptions and prevent fraud/abuse.
  • Maintain security, debug crashes, and understand product performance.
  • Respond to support requests and comply with legal obligations.

We use AI models hosted by our providers (for example, third-party large language model APIs) to generate story text from your selected mood and any optional seed you enter. Prompts and generated text may be processed by those providers under their terms and privacy policies.

4. AI-generated content

Stories are generated by AI and may be imperfect or unexpected. Do not enter sensitive personal data (passwords, health records, government IDs, precise location of others, etc.) into prompts. Content is intended for adults (18+) and is designed as soft literary / calming entertainment—not medical, therapeutic, or professional advice.

5. Legal bases (EEA/UK where applicable)

Where GDPR/UK GDPR applies, we rely on:

  • Contract — to provide the app and paid features you request.
  • Legitimate interests — to secure, improve, and understand the product.
  • Consent — where required (e.g. certain optional analytics or marketing).
  • Legal obligation — when the law requires us to retain or disclose data.

6. Sharing

We may share data with:

  • Infrastructure & AI providers that process data on our behalf to run the service.
  • Google Play for distribution, billing, and subscription management.
  • Analytics/crash tools if enabled (e.g. Firebase), under their terms.
  • Authorities when required by law or to protect rights and safety.

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising as a business model for this app.

7. Retention

We keep data only as long as needed for the purposes above. Local library items remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app (unless synced to a server account). Server logs and abuse-prevention records may be retained for a limited period. Subscription records may be kept as required for accounting and dispute resolution.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures (including HTTPS and keeping API keys off the mobile client). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

9. Children’s privacy

Nightbound is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where applicable) and is intended for users 18 years or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

10. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export your data, object to or restrict certain processing, and withdraw consent. Contact rinpan@yahoo.com. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To stop purchase-related processing, manage or cancel your subscription in Google Play (Subscriptions). Uninstalling the app removes local data from that device.

11. International transfers

Our providers may process data in countries other than yours. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. We will post the new version with an updated effective date and, if changes are material, provide additional notice in the app or store listing when appropriate.
